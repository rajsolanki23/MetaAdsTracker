## 💡 What is Creative Leaderboard in Plain English?

If you run ads on Facebook or Instagram (Meta), you know that Facebook gives you a **giant, confusing spreadsheet** with 50 columns, weird numbers, and slow loading screens. 

Trying to find out which ad is making money and which ad is losing money feels like searching for a needle in a haystack.

**Creative Leaderboard turns your ads into a live sports leaderboard.**

Instead of staring at a spreadsheet, you see your ads ranked from **#1 Best Performer** down to the worst, complete with:
- 🥇 **Gold, 🥈 Silver, and 🥉 Bronze medals** for your top ads.
- 🔥 **Flame Streaks** for ads on a winning streak.
- ❄️ **Ice Streaks** for ads that are losing money.
- 🟢 **Clear Win/Loss tags** that tell you exactly what to keep and what to turn off in 5 seconds.

## 🏆 How the System Works (The 4 Simple Game Rules)

Every time data comes in, the system automatically tags each ad with one of four badges:

| Badge | What it Means | What You Should Do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🟢 **WIN** | This ad beat your profit goal (e.g. Target ROAS of 2.5x). It is making money! | **Increase the budget / Keep it running.** |
| 🔴 **LOSS** | This ad spent your minimum budget test (e.g. $100) and **failed** to hit profit. | **Turn it off immediately to save money.** |
| 🔵 **TESTING** | This ad is brand new. It hasn't spent enough money yet to judge fairly. | **Leave it alone and let it gather data.** |
| ⚪ **PAUSED** | This ad is currently turned off or stopped. | **Archived.** |

## 🔥 What are Streaks & Movements?

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  #1 🥇 UGC Glowing Skin Video         🔥 +7d  │  ▲ +2 Rank  │
│  Spend: $320   •   Return: 4.2x ROAS  •   Cost/Sale: $18.50 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **🔥 Flame Streak (`🔥 +7d`)**:  
   This ad has been a **WINNER for 7 days in a row**. You know this is your "Superstar" ad that you can rely on.
2. **❄️ Ice Streak (`❄️ -4d`)**:  
   This ad has been **LOSING money for 4 straight days**. It's not a fluke bad day—it's burnt out. Kill it!
3. **▲ Rank Climber (`▲ +2`)**:  
   This ad jumped up 2 spots compared to yesterday. It's gaining momentum!
4. **▼ Rank Dropper (`▼ -3`)**:  
   This ad dropped 3 spots compared to yesterday. People might be getting tired of seeing it.

## 📊 The 4 Main Screens & What They Do

### 1. 🏆 The Leaderboard (`/`)
The main game screen. Shows all your ads ranked from best to worst. You can filter by Client, search by keyword (e.g. "Video" or "UGC"), or filter by Status (`WIN`, `LOSS`, `TESTING`).

### 2. 🏢 Client Portfolio View (`/clients`)
Shows high-level cards for each of your businesses:
- What is their overall profit return (Blended ROAS)?
- How much total money did they spend?
- How many Winning ads vs Losing ads do they have right now?
- 1-click on any client instantly filters the leaderboard just for them.

### 3. 📈 Creative Detail View (`/creatives/:id`)
Click on any ad row to open its 30-day performance graph.
- Shows a green line chart of its profit (ROAS) over the last month against your target goal line.
- Has a history table showing every single day's performance.
- Lets you write personal notes (e.g. *"Best hook was the first 3 seconds with founder talking"*).

### 4. 🔄 Meta Sync (`/sync`)
- Connects directly to Facebook/Meta's official API.
- Automatically refreshes all your ad data every 4 hours in the background.
- Has a **"Sync Now"** button if you want fresh numbers right this second.
